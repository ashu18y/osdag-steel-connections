\section{Introduction}
This report presents the manual verification of a beam-to-beam cover plate bolted connection designed using Osdag. All calculations are performed as per IS 800:2007 to validate the software-generated results.

\section{Input Data}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Beam Section: MB 250
    \item Material: Fe 410 ($f_y = 250$ MPa, $f_u = 410$ MPa)
    \item Factored Moment: 25 kNm
    \item Factored Shear: 15 kN
    \item Bolt Diameter: 10 mm
    \item Bolt Grade: 6.8 ($f_{ub} = 400$ MPa)
\end{itemize}

\section{Member Capacity}

\subsection{Axial Capacity}
\begin{equation}
T_d = \frac{A_g f_y}{\gamma_{m0}} = \frac{4750 \times 250}{1.1 \times 10^3} = 1079.55 \text{ kN}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Shear Capacity}
\begin{equation}
V_d = \frac{A_v f_y}{\sqrt{3} \gamma_{m0}} = 203.71 \text{ kN}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Moment Capacity}
\begin{equation}
M_d = \frac{Z_p f_y}{\gamma_{m0}} = 105.68 \text{ kNm}
\end{equation}

\section{Load Adjustment}
As per IS 800 Clause 10.7:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Adjusted Shear = 30.56 kN
    \item Adjusted Moment = 52.84 kNm
\end{itemize}

\section{Force Distribution}
Force is distributed between flange and web:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Web Force ($A_w$) = 105.85 kN
    \item Flange Force ($F_f$) = 287.24 kN
\end{itemize}

\section{Bolt Design}

\subsection{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bolts are in single shear
    \item Threads are in shear plane
    \item Partial safety factor $\gamma_{mb} = 1.25$
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Bolt Shear Capacity}
Net area of bolt, $A_{nb} = 58 \ mm^2$
\begin{equation}
V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub} \cdot A_{nb}}{\sqrt{3} \cdot \gamma_{mb}}
= \frac{400 \times 58}{\sqrt{3} \times 1.25 \times 1000}
= 16.07 \text{ kN}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Bolt Bearing Capacity}
\begin{equation}
V_{dpb} = \frac{2.5 \cdot k_b \cdot d \cdot t \cdot f_u}{\gamma_{mb}} = 59.45 \text{ kN}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Governing Capacity}
\begin{equation}
V_{db} = \min(16.07, 59.45) = 16.07 \text{ kN}
\end{equation}

\section{Plate Design Check}
Plate thickness is checked against yielding:
\begin{equation}
T = \frac{F}{b \cdot f_y}
\end{equation}
Plate is found to be safe under applied forces.

\section{Bolt Spacing Check}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Minimum pitch = $2.5d = 25$ mm
    \item Minimum edge distance = $1.5d = 15$ mm
\end{itemize}
Provided spacing satisfies IS 800 requirements.

\section{Observations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Manual results match Osdag outputs with negligible deviation.
    \item Shear governs bolt design.
    \item IS 800 provisions provide conservative estimates.
\end{itemize}

\section{Bugs and Recommendations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lack of transparency in $k_b$ calculation in Osdag
    \item Rounding differences observed in intermediate steps
    \item Recommendation: Include detailed intermediate steps in Osdag report
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
Manual verification confirms that the design generated by Osdag is accurate and follows IS 800:2007 provisions. The connection is safe under the given loading conditions.
